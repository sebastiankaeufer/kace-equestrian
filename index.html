01 — Brand Overview

The Connoisseur's
Equestrian House

KACE Equestrian is a high-end equestrian house for riders who treat the sport as a way of life — elegant, considered, and uncompromising in quality.

The brand is built for discerning clients: affluent households and dedicated enthusiasts who value craftsmanship, exclusivity, and a refined aesthetic. Every touchpoint — from the storefront to the smallest button label — should feel like an invitation into a quietly luxurious world, never loud, never casual.

02 — Voice & Tone

How KACE Speaks

The voice is the Connoisseur: elegant yet approachable, refined but never aloof. It carries the same confidence in a one-line button as in a full product story. Tone flexes with the moment — composed in everyday copy, warmer and more inviting around a launch.

Brand Voice

Refined
"Crafted in technical four-way stretch, finished by hand."
Knowledgeable
"A deeper seat and grip panel for the show ring — built for the rider who knows the difference."
Warm, not corporate
"Welcome to the stable. Let's find your fit."
Quietly confident
"Made to be worn for seasons, not trends."

Two Registers

The same voice, two settings. Everyday brand copy stays composed and editorial. The coming-soon page and launch emails lift the energy — still elegant, just warmer and more direct, with a clear call to act.

Evergreen — The Connoisseur

  • Composed, editorial, unhurried
  • Lets craftsmanship speak for itself
  • Used on product pages, about, navigation
  • Never hype, never pushy

Launch — The Invitation

  • Energetic and inviting, building anticipation
  • Short, impactful lines with one clear CTA
  • Used on coming-soon page and launch emails
  • Informal yet professional

05 — Spacing & Rhythm

Room to Breathe

Generous, consistent spacing is what makes a store feel luxurious rather than crowded. KACE works on an 8-point rhythm — every gap is a multiple of the same unit, so the whole store feels composed.

Tight · 4–8px   icon-to-label, inside small badges
Compact · 12–16px   padding inside buttons and fields
Standard · 24px   gaps between elements in a section
Comfortable · 32–48px   card padding, sub-section spacing
Spacious · 64–96px   between major page sections
Dramatic · 120px+   hero areas, footer breathing room

11 — Logo Usage

The Mark

The KACE emblem — a horse's head framed within a stirrup arch — is the brand's signature. Give it room, keep contrast high, and only ever present it in a single brand colour against a clean ground.

Stone on navy
Navy on stone
Iron on white
Don't — low contrast
✓Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the top bar.
✓Use stone or white on dark grounds; navy or iron on light grounds.
✕Place the mark on a busy photo or low-contrast colour.
✕Recolour, stretch, rotate, or add effects to the emblem.
12 — Photography & Imagery

An Editorial Eye

Imagery is where the luxury lives. Two modes: clean studio product shots for clarity, and editorial lifestyle shots for atmosphere — both kept muted, natural, and unhurried. Until KACE's own photography arrives, placeholders should already hold this restrained, premium tone.

Studio ProductClean, neutral background. Soft, even light. Product fills the frame.
Editorial LifestyleRider and horse in context — stable, arena, countryside. Natural light.
Hero & AtmosphereMoody, spacious framing for hero banners. Room for type to sit over the image.

Aspect Ratios

1:1
cards, grid
3:4

4:3

16:9

Treatment rules
Keep a consistent, restrained colour grade — warm-neutral, never over-saturated. Product images sit on white or stone; lifestyle shots may use natural settings. When text sits over an image, add a subtle navy gradient for legibility, and never crowd the frame.
